# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Curious, thoughtful cultural consumers (readers, cinephiles, intellectuals) seeking media that genuinely aligns with their core values and intellectual depth, rather than engagement-optimized dopamine bait.

## Product Purpose

Adaptive Media Engine is a cross-media discovery and recommendation platform (cinema, episodic television, literature, and critical texts). It exists to replace algorithmic rabbit holes with citation-grounded, depth-oriented recommendations that respect human attention and cultivate personal taste.

## Positioning

Unlike conventional streaming or book platforms that maximize watch time through infinite scrolling and clickbait thumbnails, Adaptive Media Engine features fixed-batch discovery, transparent machine-learning rationale citations, user-tunable preference vectors, and tactile physical-media shelf inspection.

## Operating Context

- Desktop and tablet web environments.
- High focus, reflective browsing sessions (evening study, research, intentional leisure).
- Integration with user taste logs, completion rates, and reading/viewing history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Cross-Media Taxonomy**: Unifies movies, TV shows, and books into a single coherent affinity graph.
- **Explainable Recommendations**: Every recommendation card provides citation-grounded reasoning explaining *why* it was selected based on user taste dimensions.
- **Fixed-Batch Pagination**: Zero infinite scroll; delivers curated, discrete sets to prevent cognitive fatigue.
- **Tactile 3D Shelf**: Interactive 3D physical-case and book-spine representations for immersive inspection.
- **ML Lab Telemetry**: Transparent controls for temperature, exploration vs. exploitation, and LoRA adapter weights.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Adaptive Media Engine (AME)
- **Voice**: Measured, erudite, understated, non-manipulative, respectful.
- **Visuals**: Tactile skeuomorphic physical cases, literary typography, parchment and obsidian palettes.

## Product Principles

1. **Depth Over Engagement**: Optimize for post-completion satisfaction, never mindless scroll time.
2. **Transparent Reasoning**: Always disclose why an item is recommended with verifiable rationale.
3. **Respect for Physicality**: Honor the tactile joy of physical books and cinema media in digital form.
4. **Calm Authority**: No notification spam, artificial countdowns, or gamified vanity streaks.
